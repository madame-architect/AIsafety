## Explicit linkage

The relationship describes the policy's stance toward the stated risk proposition, not whether the policy succeeds. Assessments and evidence roles below always concern the assumption as written.

- **Depends on risk premise** (`policy_depends_on`): the policy argument relies on that proposition.
- **Seeks to counter risk pathway** (`policy_counters`): the policy aims to interrupt or reduce it; success is not inferred.
- **Assesses risk premise** (`policy_assesses`): the policy or its stated rationale evaluates whether, or to what extent, it holds.

**Direction warning:** `defense_persistence` means offensive uplift survives defenses and adaptation, not that defenses remain effective. A **challenged** assessment is not evidence against safeguards and does not establish the effectiveness of any particular safeguard. No assessment is inverted or converted into a policy-effectiveness score.

## Interpretation boundary

The commitment dataset records what providers state across document editions. The evidence dataset records what empirical, operational, simulated, or modeled sources imply for stable assumptions. A link distinguishes reliance on, intended counteraction of, and assessment of a risk proposition. It does **not** say that the evidence caused the policy change, that the policy was implemented, or that the safeguard was effective. Evidence against a general risk proposition is not a control-specific effectiveness estimate.

Future provider observations append to the commitment history. Future research rounds append source observations and dated assumption assessments rather than overwriting earlier judgments.
